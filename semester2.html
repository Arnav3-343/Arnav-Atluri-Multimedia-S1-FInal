🎬 Top 3 Designs Reflection Video

In this video, I explain why I selected these three artworks as my strongest pieces of the semester. I discuss my creative process, design choices, and how I applied the medium as well as the Elements of Art and Principles of Design to my pieces: Photoshop for the animal hybrid desgins, Adobe Illustrator for the yearbook design, and Tinker CAD for the watch product design.

Digital Watch 3D Model: CAD Design

This 3D watch model demonstrates my understanding of form, structure, and digital modeling. I used proportion, symmetry, and color contrast to create a clean and readable design. The different views show how I built the watch using geometric shapes and careful alignment. The watch was intended to be designed for an audience that appreciate odd designs with cool tech capabilities.

Hybrid Animals: Photoshop

This series combines different animals into surreal hybrids using Photoshop. I focused on blending, texture matching, lighting, and color correction to make each creature look believable. I have always seen online videos of people influencers/characters sneaking into historical moments or other peoples pictures for fun. Now I can better understand how they were able to do that.

Lion–Dog Hybrid

This combination consists of the friendly appearance of a dog as well as the strong appearance of a lion through its features. This way, a realistic appearance would be achieved through adjustment of shadows, highlights, and blurring of edges.

Eagle–Cat Hybrid

In making this hybrid animal, I incorporated the aggressive nature of the eagle into a cat body. To create this piece, I employed various techniques such as masking, feather edge blending, and color correction. Through this piece of art, I became better at contrast, proportion, and compositing.

Tiger–Zebra Hybrid

This animal combination brings together the bright stripes of the zebra and the head of the tiger. In my creation, I paid special attention to ensuring that the patterns were consistent, the light was in the same direction, and the fur blends with the smooth zebra fur. I changed the black stripes of the zebra to better match the fur of the body of a tiger.

EVHS Yearbook Cover: Voltron Cougar Design

One of the shows that I enjoyed growing up is Voltron: Legendary Defender. The mechanical armor and glowing energy lines in the lions always impressed me. When I began working on my own yearbook design for Evergreen Valley High School, I tried to incorporate some of those characteristics into the design by creating an epic, robotic-looking school mascot using Adobe Illustrator. For my cougar-themed design, I used sharp geometric forms and neon lighting to create a strong, almost heroic ice type mascot with a bit of a robot vibe to it. With my project, I have had the opportunity to combine my love of nostalgic characters with modern computer skills gained from my classes this year.

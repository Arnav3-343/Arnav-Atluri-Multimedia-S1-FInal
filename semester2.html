🎬 Top 3 Designs Reflection Video

In this video, I explain why I selected these three artworks as my strongest pieces of the semester. I discuss my creative process, design choices, and how I applied the Elements of Art and Principles of Design.

Digital Watch 3D Model — CAD Design

This 3D watch model demonstrates my understanding of form, structure, and digital modeling. I used proportion, symmetry, and color contrast to create a clean and readable design. The different views show how I built the watch using geometric shapes and careful alignment.

Hybrid Animals — Photoshop Composite Series

This series combines different animals into surreal hybrids using Photoshop. I focused on blending, texture matching, lighting, and color correction to make each creature look believable. This project helped me improve my masking, layering, and digital painting skills.

Lion–Dog Hybrid

A blend of a lion’s face with a dog’s body, focusing on fur texture and lighting consistency.

Eagle–Cat Hybrid

A surreal mix of a cat and an eagle, emphasizing eye placement, beak blending, and color harmony.

Tiger–Zebra Hybrid

A combination of tiger and zebra features, using contrast and pattern alignment for realism.

EVHS Yearbook Cover — Voltron Cougar Design

This yearbook cover design uses bold color, movement, and dramatic lighting to create a futuristic, powerful representation of the EVHS cougar. I used emphasis, unity, and rhythm to guide the viewer’s eye and create a strong sense of school spirit.
